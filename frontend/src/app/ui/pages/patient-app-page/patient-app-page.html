The Virtu Patient App is a secure, mobile-first health companion that empowers patients to take full control of their medical journey. Built for iOS and Android, it unifies fragmented records, enables proactive health management, and bridges communication gaps between patients and providers. From medication reminders to virtual appointments, this intuitive tool transforms access into action—helping individuals understand their health, stay on track with treatments, and navigate care with confidence.

Designed to meet real-life challenges, the app features real-time vitals syncing, secure messaging, a smart medical card for emergencies, and robust privacy controls—giving users control without complexity.
